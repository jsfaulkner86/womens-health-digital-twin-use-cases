# KPIs and measurement

## Primary KPI
Define the single primary KPI this twin is expected to move. Make it:
- Clinically meaningful
- Measurable with available data
- Tied to a specific decision or outcome

## Secondary KPIs
List 2–3 secondary KPIs that capture:
- Operational performance (efficiency, throughput, workload)
- Equity and access (disparities, reach)
- Experience (patient, clinician, or family experience)

## Baseline and targets
Describe:
- Baseline performance (before the twin)
- Target improvement (absolute or relative)
- Time horizon for evaluation

## KPI definitions
For each KPI, specify:
- Numerator and denominator
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Data sources and time windows

## Reporting and dashboards
Explain how KPIs will be reported:
- Dashboards and views
- Frequency (monthly, quarterly)
- Who reviews them (governance, service line, execs)
