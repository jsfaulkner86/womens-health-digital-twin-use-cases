# Governance and trust

## Governance stance
Describe the governance stance for this twin:
- Scope of authority and autonomy
- Human oversight and escalation paths
- Alignment with organizational AI and data governance policies

## Risk and equity considerations
List key risks and equity concerns:
- Clinical safety and reliability
- Bias and disparities across populations
- Privacy, security, and data residency

## Controls and guardrails
Describe controls and guardrails:
- Agent budgets and limits
- Access controls and approvals
- Kill switches, rollback mechanisms, and sandbox/live separation

## Audit and traceability
Explain how activity is traced and auditable:
- Logs and event trails
- Model and agent versioning
- Replay and postmortem capabilities

## Committees and stakeholders
List governance stakeholders:
- AI/digital governance committee
- Clinical leadership and quality
- Compliance, privacy, and security

## Promotion criteria
Describe criteria for promoting from pilot/sandbox to broader deployment:
- KPI performance and safety
- Governance sign-off
- Communication and change management readiness
