# Evaluation: keep, reshape, or abandon

## Instrumentation plan
Describe how you will instrument the twin and workflows:
- Data taps (tables, feeds, events)
- Logging and tracing
- Dashboards and reports

## Review cadence
Specify how often performance is reviewed and by whom:
- Monthly, quarterly, or per pilot
- Governance committee, service line leadership, exec sponsors

## Keep criteria
Define success thresholds:
- Minimum KPI improvement
- Safety and equity criteria
- Adoption and workflow fit

## Reshape criteria
Define when to iterate instead of abandoning:
- Mixed or noisy impact on KPIs
- Low trust or adoption
- Operational or equity concerns that can be addressed

## Abandon criteria
Define clear stop rules:
- No meaningful KPI movement after defined period
- Unacceptable harms or inequities
- Misalignment with strategic priorities

## Documentation and communication
Describe how decisions (keep, reshape, abandon) are documented and communicated:
- Governance records
- Stakeholder updates
- Lessons learned
